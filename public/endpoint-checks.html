Why there is no “proxy” verdict

A BLAKE2b job may expose a short work commitment rather than a full coinbase transaction. We do not turn that commitment into a payout address. If a full transaction can be decoded, shared payout scripts still do not establish common ownership or who selected the transactions.

A public Stratum connection also says nothing conclusive about the same pool's separate miner-controlled DATUM connections. A refusal or timeout can reflect the test client, network path or authentication requirements. It is not a downtime or misconduct finding.

Each check uses a fresh throwaway payout identifier, sends subscribe and authorize messages, waits for jobs, and disconnects. No hashing, shares, payouts, account creation or private credentials are involved. This pilot is a dated snapshot, not continuous monitoring.

Beyond the endpoint: inspect actual rewards

Multiple recipients do not prove miner-controlled templates. One PyBLOCK-attributed example has three positive recipients, yet one receives about 98% of its coinbase value. Lazarus-attributed examples range from one to 97 recipients. These are useful questions to investigate, not verdicts about operators.
