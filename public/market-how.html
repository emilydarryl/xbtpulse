KEEP IT SIMPLE

A conversation before a transaction.

01

Find an offer

Check the description, XBT price, delivery region and listing date.

02

Contact the seller

Ask for evidence of the item and confirm availability. Sellers with a profile accept order requests: propose a total including delivery, timing and refund terms. Save your private order link.

03

Agree your trade

Check your saved order link for the seller’s acceptance of the exact terms. Pay directly in XBT from your own wallet and record the transaction ID. The seller checks receipt and confirmations in their wallet before fulfilling the order. The buyer confirms delivery. These are participant statements, not independent verification.
