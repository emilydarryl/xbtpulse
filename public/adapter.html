Start with the compatibility checklist

Agree on accepted-work counters, difficulty units, clock and reset behavior, found-block semantics, and which component builds templates. Pool-built Stratum jobs do not establish miner-controlled templates.

  1. Register and agree the reporting scope with a reviewer.
  2. Send sanitized snapshots and counter definitions through your private application conversation.
  3. Build and validate real interval reports locally; no token or network request is needed for validation.
  4. After approval, use your private token file to submit measurements over HTTPS.

What the kit does—and what you supply

The kit preserves one pending report before sending, refuses replacement while it is pending, and retries the exact payload. Your adapter must maintain its own durable measurement baseline and queue while delivery is blocked.

It does not read gateway status pages, logs or RPC, produce counters, prove template control, answer credential challenges or award ratings. The included example is synthetic and deliberately rejected as current telemetry. Never upload synthetic measurements to production.

No SSH access to your machines is required. Keep tokens and state outside source repositories. See the included README for permissions, retry commands and recovery steps.
