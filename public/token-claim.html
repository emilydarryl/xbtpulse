PRIVATE OPERATOR ACCESS

Get your telemetry token

This token lets your adapter send aggregate work reports to XBT Pulse. It does not give XBT Pulse access to your miners, gateway or wallet.

Checking your private link…

Generating a token replaces this provider’s previous token. Update any running adapter with the new file. This link expires after 24 hours and works once.

Save your token now

Download token.txt and place it beside your Prime adapter’s ratum.py on the operator host. Keep it private; on Linux use chmod 600 token.txt. Then follow your adapter’s submit instructions.

The token is held only in this page’s memory until you leave or clear it. XBT Pulse stores only its hash. If you leave without saving, ask for a new claim link.

Never post the private link or token in a public channel or application message. A forwarded link lets its recipient claim the token.
