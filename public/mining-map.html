HOW TO READ THE EVIDENCE

Claims, measurements, review

Operator-reported
A statement from the operator; not independently confirmed.
Supported by telemetry
Reporting supports the specific measured fact. Work reports alone do not establish an upstream connection or template independence.
Reviewed
A reviewer checked a specific claim, with its scope and date recorded. This is not a blanket endorsement.
Unknown / context only
Evidence is missing, or the connection simply explains the workflow.
