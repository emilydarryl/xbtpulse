01 / REGISTER YOUR OPERATOR

Join the telemetry program

We review submissions before enabling a provider. Registering here does not connect your gateway automatically.

Published pool profile (optional)

Public and private DATUM pools can submit details for a decentralization and transparency assessment. Private pools do not need to accept miners or disclose connection endpoints. Evidence requirements are the same for everyone; submission does not guarantee a rating.

Submit a new profile or replacement details. Include the pool profile URL in setup notes. Leave unknown fields blank.

Never include wallet keys, seed phrases, RPC credentials, or telemetry tokens here. Pending applications expire after 90 days; approved details are retained for provider administration.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

A measured approach

  1. We review your setup.Tell us which software you use and who controls the templates. A public name alone does not prove independent ownership.
  2. You receive a provider token.After review, an administrator contacts you through the details supplied. There is no automatic email or instant approval.
  3. Connect measured work.Your exporter sends accepted work and block outcomes. Depending on your software, an adapter may need to be built.

Reports are labeled operator-reported. Coverage includes participating providers only, not the whole network.
