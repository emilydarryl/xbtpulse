## Measurement definition

`summarizeTelemetry` retains its existing last-24-hour selection: intervals must fit wholly inside the window. No work is prorated across the boundary. Coverage is the union of those intervals divided by 24 hours, separately for each provider; overlapping time is counted once. The API exposes `windowStart`, `windowEnd`, `coveredMs`, `coverage` (0–1) and `reportCount`. Work/outcome aggregation and ratings are unchanged.

Coverage is not uptime, accuracy, network share, or proof that all gateways are included. A new feed naturally has partial coverage. Missing intervals remain unknown. Optional block reports do not complete work-only interval outcomes.

## Remaining evidence work

- Obtain endpoint-specific explanations for ambiguous template-control claims through the existing correction/review process. Do not infer that DATUM support means every miner controls a node.
- Preserve source dates and distinguish operator claims from independent observations. No automatic whitelist or ranking changes.
- Full network assurance still depends on the configured data source: production uses the public explorer, not an independently operated XBT node.
- An operator's automated block discovery remains their integration task; the site accepts manual or automated reports but cannot discover private mining events.
